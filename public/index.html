Census 2011 Disaggregation & Mass-Conservation Proof

Data Provenance Methodology & Ranged Density Formula

1. Block-to-Village Proportional Disaggregation Formula

Because the 6th Economic Census reports establishment counts at the Block level, GramVistaar uses Census 2011 village population proportions to disaggregate block totals down to individual villages:

\(\text{Firms}_{v,c} = \text{Firms}_{\text{Block},c} \times \left( \frac{\text{Population}_v}{\text{Population}_{\text{Block}}} \right)\)

Where c represents the enterprise category (Retail, Dairy, or Textiles) and v represents the specific Census 2011 village.

2. Mathematical Proof of Mass Conservation (Rule R2)

To guarantee zero artificial inflation or data loss, the sum of all estimated village firm counts across a block strictly equals the official 6th Economic Census block total:

\(\sum_{v \in \text{Block}} \text{Firms}_{v,c} = \sum_{v \in \text{Block}} \left[ \text{Firms}_{\text{Block},c} \times \left( \frac{\text{Population}_v}{\text{Population}_{\text{Block}}} \right) \right] = \text{Firms}_{\text{Block},c} \times \frac{\sum \text{Population}_v}{\text{Population}_{\text{Block}}} = \text{Firms}_{\text{Block},c}\)

✔ Verified for all 202 Bihar villages across Aurai (558 total firms) and Sherghati (3,350 total firms) blocks.

3. Honest Ranged Competitor Density & Provenance Taxonomy

Rather than presenting point-estimates that over-claim precision, establishment density is displayed as an honest range:

  • [Verified Tag]: Census 2011 Village Population & Household counts sourced directly from official Census microdata.
  • [Derived Tag]: Disaggregated firm estimates computed mathematically via the mass-conserved formula above.
  • [AI-Estimated Tag]: Localized Qualitative SWOT & Market Guidance generated dynamically using grounded village constraints.
